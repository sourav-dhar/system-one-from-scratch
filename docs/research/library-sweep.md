# Library sweep: articles, talks, papers and tools (2026-11-22)

> **What this is.** An automated literature and web sweep run on 2026-11-22 to find what the field guide was missing.
> It covers articles, podcasts, talks, foundational papers, new System One papers and systems, and practical resources.
> Every URL was fetched, and every arXiv id was checked against the arXiv API; anything unverifiable is marked **NO**
> in the final section. The *relevance* notes are one-line summaries, not reviews, and vendor figures quoted here are
> self-reported unless stated otherwise. Papers from this sweep are in the library index
> ([`research/papers/index.yaml`](../../research/papers/index.yaml); family `unclassified` marks System One work we
> have not yet read and placed in the taxonomy) and in the [manifest](../../research/papers/MANIFEST.md).


Verification method:
- **arXiv ids:** checked through the arXiv API, title by title (226 group-B ids plus every System One paper below). All resolved.
- **URLs:** fetched (HTTP 200 plus page title) or read with WebFetch.
- **Hacker News threads:** confirmed through the HN Algolia API.
- **YouTube:** confirmed through oEmbed.
- **DOIs:** confirmed through doi.org metadata.
- Anything I could not verify is marked **NO** and listed at the end.

**Headline findings**
1. **The index is far behind arXiv.** A sweep found about 250 System One arXiv papers since September; index.yaml holds 31. The most valuable missing ones are in table C2. They include a competing survey ("The Hitchhiker's Guide to System 1 Models", 2610.33775) and the primary paper for the Decision family (Decision 1.0, 2611.18629).
2. **Many systems are missing from §3.7 and the hosted-API notes:**
   - Fastino GLiDE (the window's most important release);
   - Baseten Mammoth-1 and Elephant-1;
   - vLLM-SR Decision 3.0 (d3);
   - Perplexity Decider v1.3;
   - Gemini Decision 1;
   - Microsoft-Decision-1.1 (GA);
   - Liquid d1.2;
   - Karpo, Piccolo, Valen, and firelex/jeff (1.6k stars; a different project from the indexed Jeff-1.1).
3. **The survey's leaderboard tables lack the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2/v2.1.** Microsoft and Liquid now quote it, and about 45% of it is private data.
4. **vLLM, SGLang and llama.cpp all ship System One endpoints** (`/v1/systemone`, `/v1/decisions`). The survey's serving sections do not mention this.
5. **The awesome-decision-models catalog has had no commits since 2026-11-13.** Its only open PR in the window adds S1CogBench, which is already indexed.
